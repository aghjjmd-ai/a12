The Bag Silhouettes Guide

Shape decides how a bag carries, what it holds and how it sits with your clothes. Choose the silhouette first; the colour can come second.

01

Structured or soft

Structured bags hold their shape on their own, look polished and protect what is inside. Soft bags drape against the body, feel relaxed and usually weigh less. Most wardrobes benefit from one of each.

03

Scale and your height

Bag size should feel in proportion to you. On a petite frame, a very large tote can overwhelm; on a tall frame, a tiny bag can look lost. Hold a bag in front of a mirror at the height it will sit before deciding.

04

Strap drop and comfort

  • Hand-held: 10–15 cm drop.
  • Shoulder: 20–28 cm, so it sits under the arm, even in a coat.
  • Crossbody: 50–60 cm, adjustable if possible.

Wider straps spread weight more comfortably than thin chains.

05

Building a small bag wardrobe

  1. A structured everyday bag in a neutral colour.
  2. A crossbody for hands-free days.
  3. An evening clutch or small top-handle.
  4. A large tote for travel or heavy days.
