About Bagello

A considered guide to the bags we carry every day.

Bagello exists for one reason: to help people choose a handbag they will still love in ten years.

We explain leathers, silhouettes, sizing and construction in calm, clear language. Our tools help you narrow your choice before you shop, and our guides explain the craft behind a well-made bag.

Independent
We do not sell bags, accept gifted products or publish paid placements.
Considered
We write about qualities that last: materials, construction and fit.
Reviewed
Every guide is read by a second editor before publication and dated.
